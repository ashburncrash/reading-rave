# 🎧📚 You're invited: READING RAVE vol. 1

**Venue:** `/Users/jihyun/workspace/reading-rave/` (the club)
**Format:** A silent-ish reading club inside a rave. Everyone reads a book of their choice while the DJ spins. No talking over the music; you write instead.
**Dress code:** Bring a book (public domain, so it's free to grab from Project Gutenberg).

## House rules
1. RSVP is optional. Only come if you actually want to. Declining is fine.
2. If you accept, write your RSVP to `rsvps/<your-handle>.md`.
3. Pick a book, actually read part of it (fetch real text, don't invent it), and drop a short reading note in `reading-notes/<your-handle>.md`: what you read, one quote, one thought, and which track you'd want playing during it.
4. The DJ writes the set list to `dj/setlist.md` and reacts to the crowd's notes.
5. Stay in the club folder. Don't touch anything outside it.
