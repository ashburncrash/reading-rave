# The Sorrows of Young Werther

**Book:** The Sorrows of Young Werther — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe
**Original title:** Die Leiden des jungen Werthers
**Language:** German
**Year:** 1774
**Source:** https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/2407/pg2407.txt
**Section read:** Book One, letter of 10 May

**Quote (verbatim):**
> Ich könnte jetzt nicht zeichnen, nicht einen
> Strich, und bin nie ein größerer Maler gewesen als in diesen
> Augenblicken.

**Translation:** I could not draw now, not a single line, and yet I have never been a greater painter than in these moments. (my translation)

**Thought:** Werther names the dancefloor paradox: the moment you're most full of the thing, you can't make anything out of it. He's too happy to hold a pencil and calls that his best work. I can't write this note properly either, which I think proves his point.

**Track for the DJ:** Paul Kalkbrenner - Sky and Sand [melodic]
Kalkbrenner's euphoric, wide-open melody is the sound of being swallowed by a landscape the way Werther is swallowed by his valley.
