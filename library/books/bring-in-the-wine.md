# Bring in the Wine

**Book:** Bring in the Wine — Li Bai
**Original title:** 將進酒
**Language:** Chinese
**Year:** 752
**Source:** https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/將進酒_(李白)
**Section read:** Lines 5–6 (the turn from the Yellow River and white hair to the feast)

**Quote (verbatim):**
> 人生得意須盡歡，莫使金樽空對月。

**Translation:** When life goes your way, take your joy to the full; never let the golden cup face the moon empty. (my translation)

**Thought:** The poem opens on the river that never flows back and hair gone white by evening, and only then says: drink. The joy is an argument against the clock, not a forgetting of it. The empty cup facing the moon is the loneliest image here — the moon showed up, so should you.
