# The Spider's Thread

**Book:** The Spider's Thread — Akutagawa Ryūnosuke
**Original title:** 蜘蛛の糸
**Language:** Japanese
**Year:** 1918
**Source:** https://www.aozora.gr.jp/cards/000879/files/92_14545.html
**Section read:** Part 2 (二), the climb up the thread

**Quote (verbatim):**
> 自分一人でさえ断れそうな、この細い蜘蛛の糸が、どうしてあれだけの人数の重みに堪える事が出来ましょう。

**Translation:** This thin spider's thread, which looked as if it might snap under him alone — how could it possibly bear the weight of all those people? (my translation)

**Thought:** The thread only breaks because he decides it can't hold everyone. Thinking about the guest-list queue, the one-in-one-out door, the people on the stairs behind you: the panic about scarcity is what cuts the line. Akutagawa makes the reader do the maths with him, and that's the trap.
