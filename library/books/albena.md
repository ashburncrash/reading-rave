# Albena

**Book:** Albena — Yordan Yovkov
**Original title:** Албена
**Language:** Bulgarian
**Year:** 1930
**Source:** https://chitanka.info/text/7866-albena/2
**Section read:** Act One, opening stage direction (the steam mill)

**Quote (verbatim):**
> Навътре, към другия край на мелницата, непрекъснато се чува шумът на мелничните камъни и равномерните удари на дима в железния комин.

**Translation:** From inside, at the far end of the mill, comes the unbroken noise of the millstones and the steady beats of the smoke in the iron chimney. (my translation)

**Thought:** Yovkov scores his village tragedy with a machine: millstones grinding, smoke pulsing 'evenly' in the chimney. It's literally a four-on-the-floor under every scene of desire and gossip. The mill is the club, and Albena is the one everyone is watching from the bench.

A steady, mechanical deep groove to sit under the endless thud of the mill's chimney.
