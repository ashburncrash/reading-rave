# Swann's Way

**Book:** Swann's Way — Marcel Proust
**Original title:** Du côté de chez Swann
**Language:** French
**Year:** 1913
**Source:** https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/2650/pg2650.txt
**Section read:** Opening of "Combray" (Part One), first paragraph

**Quote (verbatim):**
> Longtemps, je me suis couché de bonne heure. Parfois, à peine ma bougie éteinte, mes yeux se fermaient si vite que je n’avais pas le temps de me dire: «Je m’endors.»

**Translation:** For a long time, I went to bed early. Sometimes, my candle barely out, my eyes would close so quickly that I didn’t have time to tell myself: “I’m falling asleep.” (my translation)

**Thought:** The most famous opening in French is about going to bed early, read in a place where nobody ever does. Proust catches the exact seam between waking and sleep, the moment you can’t narrate. The whole seven volumes grow out of that blur.

**Track for the DJ:** Air - La femme d'argent [deep]
Slow, hazy and circling, it drifts like the half-dream state the narrator describes, consciousness dissolving before you can name it.
