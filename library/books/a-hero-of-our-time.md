# A Hero of Our Time

**Book:** A Hero of Our Time — Mikhail Lermontov
**Original title:** Герой нашего времени
**Language:** Russian
**Year:** 1840
**Source:** https://ilibrary.ru/text/12/p.24/index.html
**Section read:** Part Two, Princess Mary — journal entry «Вот уже полтора месяца...»

**Quote (verbatim):**
> Я давно уж живу не сердцем, а головою.
> Я взвешиваю, разбираю свои собственные страсти и поступки с строгим любопытством, но без участия.

**Translation:** I have long been living not with my heart but with my head. I weigh and analyse my own passions and actions with strict curiosity, but without involvement. (my translation)

**Thought:** Pechorin is the guy by the booth with his arms crossed, watching himself dance. He isn't bragging; he sounds tired. 'Strict curiosity, but without involvement' is the most honest description of a bad night out I've read.

**Track for the DJ:** Nina Kraviz - Pain in the Ass [deep]
Detached, deadpan deep house with a half-spoken vocal: the head is in the room but the heart isn't, just like Pechorin.
