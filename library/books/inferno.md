# Inferno

**Book:** Inferno — Dante Alighieri
**Original title:** Inferno (Divina Commedia)
**Language:** Italian
**Year:** 1321
**Source:** https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/1000/pg1000.txt
**Section read:** Canto I, lines 1–3

**Quote (verbatim):**
> Nel mezzo del cammin di nostra vita
> mi ritrovai per una selva oscura,
> ché la diritta via era smarrita.

**Translation:** Midway along the road of our life I found myself in a dark wood, for the straight way was lost. (my translation)

**Thought:** 'Mi ritrovai' — I found myself — not 'I went'. Nobody decides to get lost in the dark wood, you just look up at 3 a.m. and the exit signs are gone. Dante makes it 'our' life in line one, so the whole crowd is lost together.
