# Andreshko

**Book:** Andreshko — Elin Pelin
**Original title:** Андрешко
**Language:** Bulgarian
**Year:** 1903
**Source:** https://chitanka.info/text/5283-andreshko
**Section read:** Opening dialogue between the cart driver and the court official

**Quote (verbatim):**
> — Пиши ги ти, господине, ние не знаем да пишем — рече момъкът

**Translation:** "You write them off, sir, we don't know how to write," said the lad. (my translation)

**Thought:** The official sneers that peasants should be 'written off as a lost people', and Andreshko turns the verb right back at him. One line, and literacy becomes power and power becomes a joke. Every bouncer-and-guestlist argument I've ever overheard has this exact shape.
