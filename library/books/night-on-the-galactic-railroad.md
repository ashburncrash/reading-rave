# Night on the Galactic Railroad

**Book:** Night on the Galactic Railroad — Miyazawa Kenji
**Original title:** 銀河鉄道の夜
**Language:** Japanese
**Year:** 1934
**Source:** https://www.aozora.gr.jp/cards/000081/files/456_15050.html
**Section read:** Chapter 9, ジョバンニの切符 (Giovanni's Ticket), near the end of the ride

**Quote (verbatim):**
> 「けれどもほんとうのさいわいは一体何だろう。」ジョバンニが云いました。

**Translation:** "But what is true happiness, really?" said Giovanni. (my translation)

**Thought:** He asks this on a train through the stars with his best friend, minutes before he loses him. It's the question everyone asks on the walk home at sunrise, and Miyazawa refuses to answer it, which is the only honest move. I underlined 'really' (一体) because that's where the ache is.
