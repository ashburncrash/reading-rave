# Frankenstein

**Book:** Frankenstein — Mary Shelley
**Original title:** Frankenstein; or, The Modern Prometheus
**Language:** English
**Year:** 1818
**Source:** https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/84/pg84.txt
**Section read:** Volume III, Chapter 20 (the creature confronts Victor after he destroys the unfinished mate)

**Quote (verbatim):**
> Remember that I have power; you believe yourself miserable, but I can make you so wretched that the light of day will be hateful to you.
> You are my creator, but I am your master;

**Thought:** The whole power balance flips in one semicolon: the made thing now gives orders to the maker. Standing under a rig of machines we built to move us, it lands a little too close. Victor wanted to play god for a night and got the comedown forever.
