# Ten Nights of Dreams

**Book:** Ten Nights of Dreams — Natsume Sōseki
**Original title:** 夢十夜
**Language:** Japanese
**Year:** 1908
**Source:** https://www.aozora.gr.jp/cards/000148/files/799_14972.html
**Section read:** The First Night (第一夜)

**Quote (verbatim):**
> 「百年待っていて下さい」と思い切った声で云った。
> 「百年はもう来ていたんだな」とこの時始めて気がついた。

**Translation:** "Please wait for me for a hundred years," she said in a resolute voice. / "So the hundred years had already come," I realised for the first time. (my translation)

**Thought:** A hundred years passes between these two lines and he only notices once a lily opens in front of him. That's exactly how the chill-out room works: you sit down for one track and suddenly the windows are blue. The promise is kept not by counting but by forgetting to count.

**Track for the DJ:** Susumu Yokota - Song Of The Sleeping Forest [deep]
Yokota's slow, drifting loop feels like time dissolving while you wait by a grave for the sun to rise and set, over and over.
