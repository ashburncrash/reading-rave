# Botchan

**Book:** Botchan — Natsume Sōseki
**Original title:** 坊っちゃん
**Language:** Japanese
**Year:** 1906
**Source:** https://www.aozora.gr.jp/cards/000148/files/752_14964.html
**Section read:** Chapter 1, opening paragraph

**Quote (verbatim):**
> 親譲りの無鉄砲で小供の時から損ばかりしている。
> 小学校に居る時分学校の二階から飛び降りて一週間ほど腰を抜かした事がある。

**Translation:** Thanks to the recklessness I inherited from my parents, I've been losing out ever since I was a kid. Once in primary school I jumped from the second floor of the school building and couldn't stand up properly for a week. (my translation)

**Thought:** Every rave has one of these: the person who climbs the speaker stack because someone said they wouldn't dare. Sōseki makes the injury sound like a badge, and the narrator files 'losing out' as a family heirloom rather than a regret. I read this at 2am and thought of everyone I've watched land badly and grin about it.
