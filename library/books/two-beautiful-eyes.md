# Two Beautiful Eyes

**Book:** Two Beautiful Eyes — Peyo Yavorov
**Original title:** Две хубави очи
**Language:** Bulgarian
**Year:** 1910
**Source:** https://chitanka.info/text/7085-dve-hubavi-ochi
**Section read:** Final two lines (the poem's closing refrain)

**Quote (verbatim):**
> Две хубави очи. Музика, лъчи
> в две хубави очи. Душата на дете…

**Translation:** Two beautiful eyes. Music, rays of light / in two beautiful eyes. The soul of a child… (my translation)

**Thought:** Yavorov loops the opening back on itself, just reshuffled — the poem is built like a track that ends where it started. 'Music, rays' is basically a description of the strobe catching someone's face across the floor. The prayer in the middle is that the world won't ruin that look by morning.

**Track for the DJ:** Jon Hopkins - Emerald Rush [melodic]
Shimmering, looping build that feels like light caught in someone's eyes — innocence held against the coming noise.
