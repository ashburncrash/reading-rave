# Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Seas

**Book:** Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Seas — Jules Verne
**Original title:** Vingt mille lieues sous les mers
**Language:** French
**Year:** 1870
**Source:** https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/5097/pg5097.txt
**Section read:** Part One, Chapter X, "L’Homme des eaux" (Nemo declares his love for the sea)

**Quote (verbatim):**
> -- Oui ! je l'aime ! La mer est tout !
> C'est l'immense désert où l'homme n'est jamais seul, car il sent frémir la vie à ses côtés.

**Translation:** “Yes! I love it! The sea is everything! … It is the immense desert where man is never alone, for he feels life quivering all around him.” (my translation)

**Thought:** Nemo’s escape from humanity turns out to be a crowd of a different kind. "Never alone in the desert" is exactly the dancefloor at 5am: anonymous but surrounded by things that pulse. He’s the original guy who goes deep and won’t come back up.

**Track for the DJ:** Laurent Garnier - The Man with the Red Face [techno]
A long, submerged build that keeps swelling with life, the Nautilus gliding through dark water with everything quivering around it.
