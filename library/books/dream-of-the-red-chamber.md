# Dream of the Red Chamber

**Book:** Dream of the Red Chamber — Cao Xueqin
**Original title:** 紅樓夢
**Language:** Chinese
**Year:** 1791
**Source:** https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/紅樓夢/第001回
**Section read:** Chapter 1 (第一回), the couplet on the gate of the Illusory Land of Great Void in Zhen Shiyin's dream

**Quote (verbatim):**
> 假作真時真亦假，無為有處有還無。

**Translation:** When the false is taken for true, the true becomes false; where nothing is taken for something, something turns to nothing. (my translation)

**Thought:** Zhen Shiyin dozes off over a book on a hot afternoon and walks up to a stone arch carrying this couplet — the whole novel's warning, hung over a doorway. At 4am it's the realest line I know: the fog machine, the faces, the feeling that this is more true than the daylight. The book says pass through the gate anyway.
