# The Drunken Boat

**Book:** The Drunken Boat — Arthur Rimbaud
**Original title:** Le Bateau ivre
**Language:** French
**Year:** 1871
**Source:** https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/29302/pg29302.txt
**Section read:** "Bateau ivre", seventh stanza (in Poésies complètes)

**Quote (verbatim):**
> Plus fortes que l'alcool, plus vastes que vos lyres,
> Fermentent les rousseurs amères de l'amour.

**Translation:** Stronger than alcohol, vaster than your lyres, / the bitter red tides of love ferment. (my translation)

**Thought:** A sixteen-year-old telling the poets their lyres are too small. The boat has lost its rudder and gained a whole ocean of sensation, stronger than drink. That “plus fortes que l’alcool” is the line every sober raver should frame.
