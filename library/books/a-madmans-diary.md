# A Madman's Diary

**Book:** A Madman's Diary — Lu Xun
**Original title:** 狂人日記
**Language:** Chinese
**Year:** 1918
**Source:** https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/狂人日記
**Section read:** Section 10 (the madman confronts his elder brother); the line recurs in sections 3 and 10

**Quote (verbatim):**
> 『從來如此，便對麽？』

**Translation:** 'It has always been like this — does that make it right?' (my translation)

**Thought:** Four characters of tradition and then the one question nobody on the village road will answer. Lu Xun puts it in the mouth of the man everyone calls mad, which is the joke and the knife. Under a strobe it reads like a heckle at the whole room: we dance like this because we always have — fine, but is it right?
