# Rhymes

**Book:** Rhymes — Gustavo Adolfo Bécquer
**Original title:** Rimas
**Language:** Spanish
**Year:** 1871
**Source:** https://es.wikisource.org/wiki/Rimas_(B%C3%A9cquer,_1885)/Rima_LIII
**Section read:** Rima LIII ('Volverán las oscuras golondrinas'), first lines

**Quote (verbatim):**
> Volverán las oscuras golondrinas
> En tu balcón sus nidos á colgar,

**Translation:** The dark swallows will return to hang their nests on your balcony, (my translation)

**Thought:** Bécquer promises the swallows will come back, then (a few lines later) says the ones that learned our names won't. Every repeat party has this: same venue, same lineup, not the same night. The old spelling 'á colgar' in this 1885 edition feels like a vinyl crackle.

**Track for the DJ:** Nicolas Jaar - Mi Mujer [deep]
Jaar's slow, looping, half-sung groove keeps returning the way the swallows do — familiar, and a little sadder each time round.
