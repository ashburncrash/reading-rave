# The Overcoat

**Book:** The Overcoat — Nikolai Gogol
**Original title:** Шинель
**Language:** Russian
**Year:** 1842
**Source:** https://ilibrary.ru/text/980/p.1/index.html
**Section read:** Opening section [1] (Akakiy Akakievich at the department)

**Quote (verbatim):**
> «Оставьте меня, зачем вы меня обижаете?» — и в этих проникающих словах звенели другие слова: «Я брат твой».

**Translation:** "Leave me alone, why do you insult me?" — and in those piercing words other words rang out: "I am your brother." (my translation)

**Thought:** Gogol spends a page laughing at Akakiy with everybody else, then one quiet sentence flips the whole room on you. The young clerk hears 'I am your brother' under the complaint and can't un-hear it for the rest of his life. A reminder to look at the person getting jostled at the edge of the floor.
