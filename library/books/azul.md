# Azul...

**Book:** Azul... — Rubén Darío
**Original title:** Azul...
**Language:** Spanish
**Year:** 1888
**Source:** https://es.wikisource.org/wiki/El_rey_burgu%C3%A9s
**Section read:** 'El rey burgués (canto alegre)', first paragraph

**Quote (verbatim):**
> ¿Era un rey poeta? No, amigo mío: era el Rey Burgués.

**Translation:** Was he a poet king? No, my friend: he was the Bourgeois King. (my translation)

**Thought:** Darío builds a king out of pure luxury — horses, gold, fanfares — and then deflates him in one line. It's the VIP-section problem: everything money can buy except the thing that makes it sing. The poet in this story ends up freezing outside cranking a barrel organ.

**Track for the DJ:** Nicola Cruz - Colibria [afro]
Andean flutes and hand percussion over a slow pulse — the music of the street poet, not the gilded court.
