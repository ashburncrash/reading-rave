# Azaleas

**Book:** Azaleas — Kim Sowol
**Original title:** 진달래꽃
**Language:** Korean
**Year:** 1922
**Source:** https://ko.wikisource.org/wiki/진달래꽃_(시집)/진달래꽃
**Section read:** Stanza 3 (modern Korean text)

**Quote (verbatim):**
> 가시는 걸음 걸음
> 놓인 그 꽃을
> 사뿐히 즈려밟고 가시옵소서.

**Translation:** With each step as you go, / tread softly, pressing down / on the flowers laid before you. (my translation)

**Thought:** She scatters azaleas from Yaksan on the road of the one leaving her and asks him to step on them gently — devotion and accusation in the same gesture. 'Sappunhi' is a dancer's word, light-footed. Every exit from a dancefloor should feel this tender and this crushed.

**Track for the DJ:** Yaeji - Raingurl [house]
Yaeji's soft-spoken Korean-American house is both gentle and stubborn, the same way Sowol's speaker refuses to cry while laying flowers under the departing feet.
