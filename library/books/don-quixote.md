# Don Quixote

**Book:** Don Quixote — Miguel de Cervantes
**Original title:** El ingenioso hidalgo don Quijote de la Mancha
**Language:** Spanish
**Year:** 1605
**Source:** https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/2000/pg2000.txt
**Section read:** Part I, Chapter I, opening

**Quote (verbatim):**
> En un lugar de la Mancha, de cuyo nombre no quiero acordarme, no ha mucho
> tiempo que vivía un hidalgo de los de lanza en astillero, adarga antigua,
> rocín flaco y galgo corredor.

**Translation:** In a village of La Mancha, whose name I do not care to remember, there lived not long ago a gentleman of the lance-on-the-rack kind, with an old shield, a skinny nag and a racing greyhound. (my translation)

**Thought:** The most famous opening in Spanish starts by refusing to tell you where you are — like a party location that's only texted an hour before. And the inventory is all faded gear: an old shield, a skinny horse. Nobody here is a hero yet; that's the joke and the tenderness.
