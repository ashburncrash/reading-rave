# The Adventures of Pinocchio

**Book:** The Adventures of Pinocchio — Carlo Collodi
**Original title:** Le avventure di Pinocchio
**Language:** Italian
**Year:** 1883
**Source:** https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/52484/pg52484.txt
**Section read:** Chapter I, opening

**Quote (verbatim):**
> — No, ragazzi, avete sbagliato. C'era una volta un pezzo di legno.

**Translation:** — No, children, you're wrong. Once upon a time there was a piece of wood. (my translation)

**Thought:** Collodi trolls his own audience: you expect a king, you get firewood. That's the drop that refuses to drop and is better for it. A plain piece of kindling that will cry and laugh — the most ordinary material becomes the main character.
