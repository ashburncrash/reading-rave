# Alice's Adventures in Wonderland

**Book:** Alice's Adventures in Wonderland — Lewis Carroll
**Original title:** Alice's Adventures in Wonderland
**Language:** English
**Year:** 1865
**Source:** https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/11/pg11.txt
**Section read:** Chapter VI, "Pig and Pepper" (Alice meets the Cheshire Cat)

**Quote (verbatim):**
> “Oh, you can’t help that,” said the Cat: “we’re all mad here. I’m mad. You’re mad.”

**Thought:** The Cat doesn’t argue, it just includes you. That’s basically the door policy here: once you’re through, you’re already part of the madness. Alice wants a logical exit and the Cat offers belonging instead.
