# Under the Yoke

**Book:** Under the Yoke — Ivan Vazov
**Original title:** Под игото
**Language:** Bulgarian
**Year:** 1894
**Source:** https://chitanka.info/text/3753-pod-igoto
**Section read:** Part One, Chapter I ("Гост" / "A Guest"), opening paragraph

**Quote (verbatim):**
> Тая прохладна майска вечер чорбаджи Марко, гологлав, по халат, вечеряше с челядта си на двора.

**Translation:** That cool May evening Chorbadzhi Marko, bareheaded, in his dressing gown, was having supper with his household in the yard. (my translation)

**Thought:** The whole novel of revolt opens on a family dinner under the vine — bareheaded, in a robe, totally at ease. That's the calm before the drop: you need to feel the courtyard's warmth before the book takes it away. I read it on the chill-out stairs and it felt like the first minute of a set.
