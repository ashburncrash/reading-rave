# The Flowers of Evil

**Book:** The Flowers of Evil — Charles Baudelaire
**Original title:** Les Fleurs du mal
**Language:** French
**Year:** 1857
**Source:** https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Les_Fleurs_du_mal_(1861)/Correspondances
**Section read:** "Correspondances" (Spleen et Idéal, 1861 edition)

**Quote (verbatim):**
> Vaste comme la nuit et comme la clarté,
> Les parfums, les couleurs et les sons se répondent.

**Translation:** Vast as the night and as the light, / Perfumes, colours and sounds answer one another. (my translation)

**Thought:** Baudelaire basically wrote the brief for a rave: smell, light and sound all answering each other in one dark, deep unity. Under strobes and smoke, synaesthesia stops being a theory. The poem is a temple; so is the warehouse.

**Track for the DJ:** Agoria - Scala [melodic]
Lush French melodic techno where the synth colours and the vocal seem to answer each other, sensations echoing from far away into one unity.
