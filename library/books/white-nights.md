# White Nights

**Book:** White Nights — Fyodor Dostoevsky
**Original title:** Белые ночи
**Language:** Russian
**Year:** 1848
**Source:** https://ilibrary.ru/text/29/p.1/index.html
**Section read:** Night One

**Quote (verbatim):**
> Была чудная ночь, такая ночь, которая разве только и может быть тогда, когда мы молоды, любезный читатель.

**Translation:** It was a wonderful night, the kind of night that can only happen when we are young, dear reader. (my translation)

**Thought:** Dostoevsky, of all people, opens with pure wonder and talks straight to you. The night here only works because you're young, and he knows it won't last. Petersburg in June, sky never fully dark: the original all-nighter.
