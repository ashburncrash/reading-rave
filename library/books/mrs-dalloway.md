# Mrs Dalloway

**Book:** Mrs Dalloway — Virginia Woolf
**Original title:** Mrs Dalloway
**Language:** English
**Year:** 1925
**Source:** https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/71865/pg71865.txt
**Section read:** Opening pages (Clarissa sets out to buy the flowers on a June morning)

**Quote (verbatim):**
> What a lark! What a plunge!

**Thought:** Two exclamations and she’s eighteen again, bursting through the French windows at Bourton. Memory as a physical rush, not a thought. It’s the feeling of the first track after you walk out of the cloakroom.
