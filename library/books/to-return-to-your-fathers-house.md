# To Return to Your Father's House

**Book:** To Return to Your Father's House — Dimcho Debelyanov
**Original title:** Да се завърнеш в бащината къща
**Language:** Bulgarian
**Year:** 1912
**Source:** https://chitanka.info/text/6161
**Section read:** Opening two lines (first stanza)

**Quote (verbatim):**
> Да се завърнеш в бащината къща,
> когато вечерта смирено гасне

**Translation:** To return to your father's house / when the evening is meekly fading out (my translation)

**Thought:** The whole poem is one long infinitive — a wish, never an action — and the last couplet admits it's in vain. That's the 6 a.m. feeling exactly: the music stops and you think of home. Debelyanov died at the front four years later, which makes the 'meekly fading' evening hard to read.
