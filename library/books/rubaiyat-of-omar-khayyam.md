# Rubaiyat of Omar Khayyam

**Book:** Rubaiyat of Omar Khayyam — Omar Khayyam
**Original title:** رباعیات عمر خیام
**Language:** Persian
**Year:** 1120
**Source:** https://fa.wikisource.org/wiki/رباعیات_عمر_خیام/ای_کرده_ز_لطف_و_قهر_تو_صنع_خدا
**Section read:** Quatrain 'ای کرده ز لطف و قهر تو صنع خدا' (lines 3–4), from an 18th-century manuscript

**Quote (verbatim):**
> بزم تو بهشت است مرا جرمی نیست
> چون است که در بهشت ره نیست مرا

**Translation:** Your feast is paradise, and I have done no wrong — / so how is it there's no way into paradise for me? (my translation)

**Thought:** Khayyam calls God's banquet paradise and then stands outside the door asking why he's not on the list. It's the most rave line in Persian: the party is heaven, I'm innocent, why am I stuck in the queue? The cheek of it — arguing with eternity like a bouncer.
