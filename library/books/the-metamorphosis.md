# The Metamorphosis

**Book:** The Metamorphosis — Franz Kafka
**Original title:** Die Verwandlung
**Language:** German
**Year:** 1915
**Source:** https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/22367/pg22367.txt
**Section read:** Chapter I, opening sentence

**Quote (verbatim):**
> Als Gregor Samsa eines Morgens aus unruhigen Träumen erwachte, fand er
> sich in seinem Bett zu einem ungeheueren Ungeziefer verwandelt.

**Translation:** As Gregor Samsa woke one morning from restless dreams, he found himself transformed in his bed into a monstrous vermin. (my translation)

**Thought:** This is exactly the 6 a.m. feeling of walking out of the warehouse: you went in a person and came out with a carapace. What gets me is "unruhigen Träumen" — the dreams were already uneasy, so the change isn't a shock, it's a sequel. Kafka doesn't explain anything; he just reports, the way a kick drum does.

**Track for the DJ:** Kraftwerk - The Robots [techno]
A deadpan, mechanical voice announcing a new body — Kraftwerk's flat 'we are the robots' matches Kafka's flat report of becoming something else.
