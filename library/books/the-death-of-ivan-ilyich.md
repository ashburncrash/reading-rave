# The Death of Ivan Ilyich

**Book:** The Death of Ivan Ilyich — Leo Tolstoy
**Original title:** Смерть Ивана Ильича
**Language:** Russian
**Year:** 1886
**Source:** https://ilibrary.ru/text/7/p.12/index.html
**Section read:** Chapter XII (the final chapter)

**Quote (verbatim):**
> Он искал своего прежнего привычного страха смерти и не находил его.
> Вместо смерти был свет.

**Translation:** He searched for his former habitual fear of death and could not find it. In place of death there was light. (my translation)

**Thought:** Three days of screaming, and then the fear just isn't where he left it — like patting your pocket for your phone at 5am and finding nothing, and feeling lighter. Tolstoy gives the whole revelation a single short line, no adjectives. It's the moment the strobes cut and the room goes white.
