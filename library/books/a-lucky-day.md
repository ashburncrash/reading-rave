# A Lucky Day

**Book:** A Lucky Day — Hyun Jin-geon
**Original title:** 운수 좋은 날
**Language:** Korean
**Year:** 1924
**Source:** https://ko.wikisource.org/wiki/운수_좋은_날
**Section read:** The ending (Kim Cheomji returns home with the seolleongtang)

**Quote (verbatim):**
> “설렁탕을 사다놓았는데 왜 먹지를 못하니, 왜 먹지를 못하니... 괴상하게도 오늘은! 운수가 좋더니만... ”

**Translation:** "I bought you seolleongtang, why can't you eat it, why can't you eat it... how strange, today! And my luck was so good..." (my translation)

**Thought:** All day the rickshaw man kept winning fares and kept stalling his way home, drinking because the luck felt too good to be safe. The soup he promised arrives and his wife is gone. The repetition is the whole tragedy — he keeps saying the same thing as if the loop could change the ending.

**Track for the DJ:** Peggy Gou - It Makes You Forget (Itgehane) [house]
A bright, looping Korean-titled house tune about forgetting — exactly what Kim Cheomji tries to do in the tavern, and exactly what the last page won't let him.
