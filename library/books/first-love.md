# First Love

**Book:** First Love — Ivan Turgenev
**Original title:** Первая любовь
**Language:** Russian
**Year:** 1860
**Source:** https://ilibrary.ru/text/1335/p.9/index.html
**Section read:** Chapter IX

**Quote (verbatim):**
> а я сидел, глядел, слушал и наполнялся весь каким-то безыменным ощущением, в котором было всё: и грусть, и радость, и предчувствие будущего, и желание, и страх жизни.

**Translation:** and I sat, looked, listened, and filled up entirely with some nameless feeling that held everything: sadness and joy, a premonition of the future, desire, and the fear of life. (my translation)

**Thought:** Sixteen, legs hanging off a broken greenhouse wall, and he feels everything at once without a name for any of it. Then the last line of the paragraph gives it one name anyway: Zinaida. That's the chill-out room at sunrise, crushing on someone you met three hours ago.
