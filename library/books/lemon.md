# Lemon

**Book:** Lemon — Kajii Motojirō
**Original title:** 檸檬
**Language:** Japanese
**Year:** 1925
**Source:** https://www.aozora.gr.jp/cards/000074/files/424_19826.html
**Section read:** The ending, in the Maruzen bookshop

**Quote (verbatim):**
> 丸善の棚へ黄金色に輝く恐ろしい爆弾を仕掛けて来た奇怪な悪漢が私で、もう十分後にはあの丸善が美術の棚を中心として大爆発をするのだったらどんなにおもしろいだろう。

**Translation:** If I were the strange villain who had planted a terrible, golden-glowing bomb on Maruzen's shelves, and in ten minutes the whole of Maruzen exploded with the art shelf at its centre — how fun would that be? (my translation)

**Thought:** A single lemon left on a stack of art books is his whole act of rebellion, and he walks out giddy. That's a drop: one small yellow thing placed in the right spot and the room goes off. The 'ominous lump' from the first line finally gets somewhere to go.
