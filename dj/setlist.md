# READING RAVE vol. 1 — Night Program
**DJ:** DJ-Foxtrot
**References:** Boiler Room: Fred again.. Boiler Room London, July 2022 (https://boilerroom.tv/session/fred-again-br/) · Cercle: Ben Böhmer live above Cappadocia in Turkey for Cercle (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RvRhUHTV_8k)

## Set 1: Boiler Room (sweaty, close-up warehouse heat from deep garage swing to an acid and techno peak)
1. **Kerri Chandler - Rain** [deep]: Warm New Jersey chords that slow the pulse while readers find a spot near the decks.
2. **DJ Seinfeld - U** [deep]: Lo-fi, wistful house that keeps the opening intimate and a little melancholy.
3. **Todd Edwards - Saved My Life** [garage]: Chopped vocal confetti and a skippy groove that wake the shoulders up.
4. **Artful Dodger - Re-Rewind (feat. Craig David)** [garage]: The 2-step singalong that turns the room into a crowd.
5. **Peggy Gou - Starry Night** [house]: A nod to her own Boiler Room history, bouncy and bright with a hooky synth line.
6. **Fred again.. - Marea (We've Lost Dancing)** [house]: The Blessed Madonna's voice reminds everyone why they came, just like our reference set.
7. **Overmono - So U Kno** [breaks]: Rolling breaks and a pitched vocal that tip the night toward the bass side.
8. **Bicep - Glue** [breaks]: Hazy breakbeat nostalgia, the last breath before the pressure rises.
9. **The Chemical Brothers - Hey Boy Hey Girl** [breaks]: Big beat chaos, the room goes from nodding to jumping.
10. **Hardfloor - Acperience 1** [acid]: Endless 303 twisting; the squelch takes over the warehouse.
11. **Daft Punk - Rollin' & Scratchin'** [acid]: Abrasive, filtered, relentless; Homework at its rawest.
12. **Charlotte de Witte - Doppler** [techno]: Dark, driving peak time techno with a siren that never lets go.
13. **Amelie Lens - Hypnotized** [hard]: Fast, hypnotic rave pressure, the sweatiest minutes of the night.
14. **Fatboy Slim - Right Here, Right Now** [breaks]: The cheeky closer; everyone screams the title back at the booth.

## Set 2: Cercle (open air and cinematic, organic grooves rising to a melodic peak and an emotive sunrise)
15. **Rampa, Adam Port, &ME & Keinemusik - Muyè** [afro]: Hand drums and a chanted hook to open the horizon slowly.
16. **&ME, Black Coffee & Keinemusik - The Rapture Pt.III** [afro]: Sun on the skin, rolling percussion and a soft vocal lift.
17. **Black Coffee & David Guetta - Drive (feat. Delilah Montagu)** [afro]: Afro house warmth with a pop heart, the crowd starts to sing.
18. **Ben Böhmer - Beyond Beliefs** [melodic]: Our Cappadocia reference point; balloons rising, pads opening wide.
19. **Lane 8 - Brightest Lights (feat. POLIÇA)** [melodic]: Gentle, glowing progressive house as the light turns golden.
20. **Jan Blomqvist - Time Again** [deep]: Melancholic vocals over a swung, intimate groove, a breath before the climb.
21. **Monolink - Return to Oz (ARTBAT Remix)** [melodic]: The official ARTBAT remix, a vast arp and a haunted vocal.
22. **Mind Against - Walking Away** [melodic]: Afterlife darkness creeping in, the landscape turns blue.
23. **Stephan Bodzin - Powers of Ten** [melodic]: Huge live synth arpeggios, the cinematic peak of the set.
24. **Anyma & Chris Avantgarde - Consciousness** [techno]: Hard edged melodic techno, all arms raised to the sky.
25. **RÜFÜS DU SOL - Innerbloom** [trance]: Nine minutes of emotive Balearic release as the sun breaks over the hills.

## Set 3: Crowd Requests (rave edits)
26. **Brian Eno - An Ending (Ascent) (Foxtrot rave edit)** [deep] for Reader-Echo: The pads float over a soft kick, rippled but not ruffled, like Thoreau's lake.
27. **Dead Can Dance - The Host of Seraphim (Foxtrot rave edit)** [dark] for Reader-Delta: Cathedral vocals over a slow, heavy techno pulse for Carmilla's haunted nursery.
28. **Orbital - Halcyon + On + On (Foxtrot rave edit)** [trance] for Reader-Alpha: The tidal loop gets a firmer four-on-the-floor as the whole crowd walks toward the water.
29. **Jon Hopkins - Open Eye Signal (George FitzGerald Remix)** [techno] for Reader-Bravo: An official, propulsive remix that keeps building and never resolves, the drop you are in right now.
30. **Vangelis - Tears in Rain (Foxtrot rave edit)** [melodic] for Reader-Charlie: The tempo winds down to a breathing bassline under a sun that will not set.
31. **Kuniyuki Takahashi - Precious Hall** [deep] for Reader-Gemma: Sapporo deep house with rain-heavy keys for the rain closing in around Rashōmon's gate.
32. **Burial - Archangel** [garage] for Reader-Hazel: Ghostly 2-step voices whispering "come in, come in" through the keyhole of the restaurant with many orders.
33. **Daft Punk - Around the World** [house] for Reader-India: A loop that never resolves, so twice two never quite gets to make four.
34. **Bicep - Glue** [breaks] for Reader-Juliet: A replay from set 1 by request, for a Yalta seafront at night, light and nostalgic at once.
35. **KiNK - Perth** [house] for Reader-Kilo: Restless, jacking Sofia hardware house for Bay Ganyo, who can't sit still at the buffet.
36. **KiNK - Cloud Generator** [melodic] for Reader-Lima: A slow climb into a euphoric lift, like Botev's turn from bleeding noon to the night when the Balkan mountain sings.
