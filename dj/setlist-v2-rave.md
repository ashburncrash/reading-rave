# READING RAVE vol. 1 — Rave Set
**DJ:** DJ-Foxtrot
**Arc:** Chicago house warm-up → acid and big-room build → techno and rave peak → Balearic trance sunrise

## Warm-up (deep house, Chicago house)
1. **Mr. Fingers - Can You Feel It**: The deep house prayer that opens the doors and gets the room swaying while the first pages turn.
2. **Frankie Knuckles - Your Love**: That glistening arpeggio and vocal pull everyone off the walls and onto the floor.

## Build (acid house, big-room breaks)
3. **Phuture - Acid Tracks**: The 303 starts to squelch and the groove starts to bite.
4. **Underworld - Born Slippy .NUXX**: Rolling breaks and a shouted vocal that turn a reading room into a rave.
5. **Faithless - Insomnia**: The slow-burn stab riff that every hand in the room is waiting for.

## Peak time (acid techno, Detroit techno, hardcore rave)
6. **Josh Wink - Higher State of Consciousness**: A screaming acid line that sends the floor through the roof.
7. **Jeff Mills - The Bells**: Relentless Detroit techno, all hands up, no let-up.
8. **The Prodigy - Out of Space**: Breakbeat hardcore at full throttle, the loudest moment of the night.

## Sunrise (Balearic trance, euphoric trance)
9. **Energy 52 - Café del Mar**: Shimmering trance chords as the first light hits the windows.
10. **Chicane - Offshore**: A warm Balearic glide back to shore, eyes half closed, books open again.

## Set 2: Crowd requests (rave edits)
11. **Brian Eno - An Ending (Ascent) (Foxtrot rave edit)** [warm-up] for Reader-Echo: The pads float over a soft kick, rippled but not ruffled, like Thoreau's lake.
12. **Dead Can Dance - The Host of Seraphim (Foxtrot rave edit)** [dark] for Reader-Delta: Cathedral vocals over a slow, heavy techno pulse for Carmilla's haunted nursery.
13. **Orbital - Halcyon + On + On (Foxtrot rave edit)** [build] for Reader-Alpha: The tidal loop gets a firmer four-on-the-floor as the whole crowd walks toward the water.
14. **Jon Hopkins - Open Eye Signal (George FitzGerald Remix)** [peak] for Reader-Bravo: An official, propulsive remix that keeps building and never resolves, the drop you are in right now.
15. **Vangelis - Tears in Rain (Foxtrot rave edit)** [sunrise] for Reader-Charlie: The tempo winds down to a breathing bassline under a sun that will not set.
