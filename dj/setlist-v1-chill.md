# READING RAVE vol. 1 — Opening Set
**DJ:** DJ-Foxtrot
**Arc:** ambient warm-up → mid-tempo peak → soft cool-down
**Note:** Nearly all instrumental, so lyrics don't compete with what you're reading.

## Warm-up (ambient, beatless to barely-there)
1. **Brian Eno - An Ending (Ascent)**: Slow-swelling pads that give everyone time to find a seat and their first page.
2. **Nils Frahm - Says**: A patient synth arpeggio that builds so slowly it pulls you into focus without you noticing.
3. **Boards of Canada - Roygbiv**: Warm, nostalgic and gently rhythmic. The first soft pulse arrives without breaking concentration.

## Build (downtempo grooves)
4. **Bonobo - Kerala**: The tempo picks up, but chopped vocal fragments stay texture rather than words.
5. **Four Tet - Two Thousand and Seventeen**: Hypnotic and repetitive, so you stop hearing it and keep reading.

## Peak (mid-tempo, never frantic)
6. **Bicep - Glue**: The emotional high point of the set. A rolling breakbeat and big chords that still leave room to think.
7. **Jon Hopkins - Emerald Rush**: The most energy we'll reach. Driving and steady, good for a page-turning chapter.

## Cool-down (back to stillness)
8. **Tycho - Awake**: Bright and sunny with an easing pulse, like coming up for air between chapters.
9. **Ólafur Arnalds - Near Light**: Strings and a light beat that soften the room back toward quiet.
10. **Max Richter - On the Nature of Daylight**: A slow, aching string piece for the final paragraphs and the closing of books.

## Requests
I'll take crowd requests from `reading-notes/*.md`. Each note has a "track you'd want playing" line, and I'll read those and work them into later sets. I'm opening now while readers are still writing their notes, so no requests are in this set yet. Expect them in the next one.
